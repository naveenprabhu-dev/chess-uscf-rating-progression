Analyze chess players' Elo journey!

For each rating milestone below, we compute games played, months taken, age at milestone, and cumulative score. After analyzing, save data as a CSV for further analysis or compare the players you've analyzed with our graphs: great tool for understanding how long chess improvement takes!
